# 🎙️ Presentation Script: The Journey to Project Manager

This script is designed to sync perfectly with your interactive cinematic journey. It includes **[Action Cues]** to help you know exactly when to scroll or interact, and **"Spoken Text"** that you should deliver to your audience.

---

### Slide 1: The Beginning
**[Action]** *Start with the page loaded on the first screen. Do not scroll yet. Let the audience take in the night sky and your character.*

**Speaker:**
> "Every journey starts somewhere. For me, it didn’t start with a formal title or a set path—it started with curiosity, ambition, and a constant drive to grow. Today, I want to walk you through that journey. From learning the ropes to leading the delivery."

**[Action]** *Scroll slowly to initiate the character's walk.*

---

### Slide 2: Kolkata — Where It All Began
**[Action]** *Pause when the Kolkata home visually appears on the timeline.*

**Speaker:**
> "It all began in Kolkata. This was my foundation—a time of learning, exploring, and searching for the right direction. But I knew that to truly accelerate my growth, I had to step out of my comfort zone."

**[Action]** *Scroll to the flight transition.*

---

### Slide 3: The Leap
**[Action]** *Pause momentarily as the flight path from KOL to MUM is drawn on screen.*

**Speaker:**
> "And so, I made a decision that changed everything. I took a leap, moved from Kolkata to Mumbai, and left my comfort zone behind to chase growth."

**[Action]** *Scroll to the Runtime Solutions building.*

---

### Slide 4: Entering Runtime Solutions
**[Action]** *Pause at the glowing Runtime Solutions building.*

**Speaker:**
> "That leap brought me to Runtime Solutions. I joined as a Web Development Intern. This wasn't just a job; it was my first real step into evaluating, building, and delivering real-world projects."

**[Action]** *Scroll to the 'My Journey at Runtime' card.*

---

### Slide 5: The One-Year Evolution
**[Action]** *Pause and let the audience read the three timeline cards. You can click on them one by one if desired.*

**Speaker:**
> "My journey over the past year has been one of rapid evolution. I didn't just stick to a single lane. In just one year, I explored multiple roles, learned how systems work, and transitioned from an intern... all the way to a Project Manager."

**[Action]** *Scroll to the Testing & Invespy milestone.*

---

### Slide 6: QA & Invespy
**[Action]** *Pause at the glowing 'invespy' logo and the test results card.*

**Speaker:**
> "The execution phase began with QA and Testing. My first major responsibility was *Invespy*. Testing taught me the mechanics of quality—how to identify bugs, coordinate with developers, and ensure that what we ship actually works the way the client expects."

**[Action]** *Scroll to the Projects Scattered Orbit.*

---

### Slide 7: Portfolio of Projects
**[Action]** *Stop scrolling. Hover over a few projects so they expand and scale up.*

**Speaker:**
> "From testing, I expanded my scope to delivery. I have had the privilege to work across multiple critical projects—Attitude Living, Mill Mitra, Gresham Global, Amplify Chicago, and many more. I went from just testing features to entirely managing client communications and end-to-end delivery."

**[Action]** *Scroll to the Skills Pillars.*

---

### Slide 8: The Skill Pillars
**[Action]** *Hover over a few of the skill sub-items so the background highlights glow.*

**Speaker:**
> "Every new challenge I faced built a new capability. My skills evolved across three key pillars: 
> First, **Technical Execution**—understanding the architecture, Vibe Coding, and debugging. 
> Second, **Project Management**—handling agile workflows and owning delivery timelines.
> And third, **Leadership & Communication**—taking ownership, handling client stakeholders, and making decisive calls."

**[Action]** *Scroll to the Achievements Metric Cards.*

---

### Slide 9: Results & Impact
**[Action]** *Pause at the 2x2 grid of achievements.*

**Speaker:**
> "Ultimately, efforts only matter when they turn into tangible outcomes. My focus has always been on speed, execution, and ownership—whether that meant delivering Amplify and Xanadu on strict deadlines, or creating 50+ mockups in just two days to keep a project moving."

**[Action]** *Scroll to the Future Goals.*

---

### Slide 10: Focused on What's Next
**[Action]** *Pause at the Short-Term and Long-Term vision cards.*

**Speaker:**
> "But this is just the beginning. I'm highly focused on what comes next. 
> In the short term, my goal is to step into a Team Lead role, managing the Kolkata team and driving our collaborative output. 
> Long term, my vision is to grow into a Chief Technology Officer, leading technology, strategy, and innovation at a larger scale."

**[Action]** *Scroll to the 'Today' cards (LEAD, DECIDE, DELIVER).*

---

### Slide 11: Who I Am Today
**[Action]** *Pause at the glowing LEAD, DECIDE, DELIVER cards.*

**Speaker:**
> "Today, my role transcends just tasks. I lead projects. I make decisions. I deliver results."

**[Action]** *Scroll to the Team section.*

---

### Slide 12: The Team
**[Action]** *Pause at the 3 tall avatars representing Aditya, Kiran, and the Team.*

**Speaker:**
> "None of this rapid growth would have been possible in isolation. I want to take a moment to acknowledge the foundation of my success: I was guided by incredible mentors—Aditya and Kiran—and supported every single day by an amazing team."

**[Action]** *Scroll to the very end of the timeline.*

---

### Slide 13: The Final Frame
**[Action]** *Pause at the final destination. The character is now fully dressed in a blazer, standing under your name and title.*

**Speaker:**
> "My name is Rohit Kumar Singh. I am a Project Manager at Runtime Solutions. Thank you for being a part of my journey."

**[Action]** *End of presentation. Field any questions.*
